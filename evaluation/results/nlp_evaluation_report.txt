--- STEP 5: Aspect-Based Sentiment Analysis (ABSA) Reality Check ---
  [CRITIQUE OF MANUSCRIPT CLAIMS]:
  The manuscript claims an 'Aspect-Based Semantic Sentiment Modeling' pipeline.
  Inspection of rec_backend/data_processor.py shows that aspect assignment is performed by:
    - Regular expression keyword matching (e.g. clean, dirty, toilet, on-time).
    - Overall sentence polarity computed via TextBlob lexicon.
  LIMITATION:
  TextBlob assigns sentence-level polarity and cannot distinguish conflicting aspect polarities
  within a single sentence (e.g., 'Food was delicious but the train was terribly late').
  The manuscript must clarify this as a lexicon-guided aspect extraction framework,
  not a fine-tuned deep neural ABSA network.
